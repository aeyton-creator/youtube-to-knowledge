# Coal

Still the largest source of electricity generation globally, driven by China and India. Two families: **thermal (steam) coal** for power and **metallurgical (coking) coal** for steelmaking.

Contract size on ICE coal futures: **1,000 t/month**.

## Fundamentals

- **Supply:** China (~50% of world output, mostly consumed domestically), India, Indonesia (largest thermal exporter), Australia (largest met coal exporter), Russia, South Africa, Colombia, US.
- **Demand:** Chinese and Indian power generation; China's hydro output and summer heat swing import needs. Europe is in structural decline but flexes with gas prices.
- Chinese domestic policy (safety inspections, output targets, import quotas, port restrictions) is the dominant price driver.
- Met coal follows Chinese and Indian steel output; Queensland weather (wet season Dec–Mar) disrupts supply.

## Trading notes

- In Europe, coal vs gas is assessed through the **clean dark spread** (power − coal − carbon) vs the **clean spark spread** (power − gas − carbon).
- Newcastle–API2 spread reflects Pacific vs Atlantic balance and freight.
- Calorific-value adjustments matter: lower-CV coal trades at a discount roughly proportional to energy content but with additional quality penalties (ash, sulfur, moisture).
