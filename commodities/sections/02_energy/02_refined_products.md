# Refined Products

Refinery margins connect crude to consumers. Products often lead crude at turning points because they reflect end-user demand.

## Refining basics

- **Simple (hydroskimming)** refineries distil crude; **complex** refineries add FCC, hydrocrackers and cokers to convert heavy residue into light products.
- Typical US Gulf yields: ~45% gasoline, ~30% distillate, ~10% jet, remainder LPG, resid, coke.
- **Nelson Complexity Index** measures refinery sophistication.

## Gasoline

- US is ~45% of global gasoline demand; peak demand Memorial Day–Labor Day.
- **RVP seasonality:** summer-grade (low vapour pressure) gasoline from April onwards costs more; RBOB contract months switch spec (April–September contracts are summer grade). This creates a jump in the Mar/Apr spread.
- Ethanol blending (~10% in US) links RBOB to corn and RINs (D6).

## Distillates (diesel, gasoil, heating oil, jet)

- Most economically sensitive barrel: trucking, rail, agriculture, mining, shipping, heating.
- Europe is structurally short diesel and historically imported from Russia; post-2023 EU embargo, it imports from the US Gulf, Middle East and India.
- Winter heating demand (US Northeast, Europe) and low stocks cause sharp backwardations.
- **IMO 2020** (0.5% sulfur cap for marine fuel) raised demand for low-sulfur blends and widened the hi-5 spread (HSFO vs VLSFO).
- Jet fuel tracks air travel; it trades at a premium/discount to diesel.

## Fuel oil and residuals

- HSFO (3.5% S) demand comes from scrubber-fitted ships and Middle East power burn in summer.
- VLSFO (0.5% S) is the main marine fuel; Singapore and Fujairah are bunker hubs.

## Key drivers

1. Refinery outages and run rates (EIA weekly utilisation).
2. Product inventories vs 5-year range (EIA, ARA Insights Global, Singapore IES).
3. Export arbitrage flows (US Gulf to LatAm/Europe; East of Suez to Europe).
4. Weather (heating degree days), freight (diesel trucking), hurricane hits on Gulf Coast refineries.
5. Structural capacity: closures in OECD vs new mega-refineries in Asia/Middle East/Africa (e.g., Dangote in Nigeria, ramping since 2024).
