## Supply

- China (Xinjiang), India, **Brazil (now #1 exporter)**, US (#2 exporter; Texas dryland is weather-critical), Pakistan, Australia, Uzbekistan, Turkey.
- US calendar: plant Apr–Jun, boll set Jul–Aug, harvest Sep–Dec. Hurricanes affect Southeast/Delta crops.
- **Texas West Plains abandonment** (dryland drought) can swing the US crop by millions of bales.

## Demand

- Mill use: China, India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Turkey. Demand follows apparel retail and the macro cycle.
- **US Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act** bans Xinjiang cotton in US supply chains — reshapes trade flows.
- Polyester price (via crude/PTA) affects fibre substitution.

## Price drivers

1. US weather and crop conditions; USDA weekly export sales (key every Thursday).
2. Chinese reserve policy and import quotas.
3. Global textile demand, retail inventories.
4. **On-call sales/purchases** report (CFTC weekly): unfixed mill purchases are latent futures buying.
5. Certified stocks.
