# Sugar

~180 Mt produced annually; ~80% from sugarcane, ~20% from sugar beet. The market is shaped by Brazil's choice between making sugar or ethanol, and by Indian policy.

**White premium = No.5 ($/t) − No.11 (¢/lb × 22.0462)**. A premium above ~$100–110/t typically makes refining raw sugar profitable for standalone refiners.

## Ethanol parity

- Brazilian mills compare sugar prices with **hydrous ethanol** prices (set by Petrobras gasoline pricing, BRL, and the flex-fuel fleet). Sugar must exceed ethanol parity for mills to maximise sugar.
- Higher crude/gasoline prices → more cane to ethanol → bullish sugar. Brazil's ethanol blend in gasoline (E30 from 2025) adds demand.

## Price drivers

1. Brazilian CS weather (rain, frost, fire risk in dry season) and cane yield (ATR, TRS per tonne).
2. UNICA sugar mix and crush pace.
3. Indian monsoon, cane area, export policy and ethanol diversion.
4. Thai production.
5. BRL, crude oil.
6. Import demand (China, Indonesia, Middle East refiners).

## Trading notes

- Spreads: March/May and the **White premium**. V (Oct) expiry delivery size signals physical demand.
- Raw sugar delivery tonnage at expiry and the receiver's identity are watched closely.
