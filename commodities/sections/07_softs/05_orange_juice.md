# Frozen Concentrated Orange Juice (FCOJ)

A tiny, illiquid market with extreme volatility. Brazil (São Paulo) produces most of the world's orange juice; Florida output has collapsed due to citrus greening and hurricanes.

## Fundamentals

- **Supply:** Brazil (~70%+ of world exports; Fundecitrus crop estimates in May), Florida (greening disease — HLB — cut output by ~90% from peak), Mexico.
- Hurricanes (Florida, Jun–Nov), freezes (Dec–Feb), Brazil drought.
- **Demand:** US, EU; declining per-capita consumption; price-elastic at high levels.
- Large price swings with low liquidity — wide bid/offer and daily limits.
