# Rice, Oats & Minor Grains

## Rice

- ~520 Mt (milled) produced; only ~10% traded — a thin international market with heavy government involvement.
- Exporters: **India (~40% of trade)**, Thailand, Vietnam, Pakistan, US, Myanmar. India's export bans/duties (2022–2024, then lifted 2024–25) move world prices sharply.
- Benchmarks: Thai 5% broken FOB, Vietnam 5% broken, Indian parboiled.
- **CBOT Rough Rice (ZR):** 2,000 cwt (hundredweight), $0.005/cwt = $10; US long-grain (Arkansas). Illiquid.
- Drivers: monsoon (India), El Niño, government stockholding, Philippines/Indonesia import tenders.

## Oats

- **CBOT Oats (ZO):** 5,000 bu, ¼¢; 1 bu = 32 lb. Supply mainly from Canada (Prairies) and Nordics; demand from food (oat milk, cereals) and feed/horse. Very thin, prone to squeezes.

## Barley and sorghum

- Barley: feed and malting; EU, Russia, Australia, Canada; China is a major importer. Traded mostly physically.
- Sorghum: US and Argentina export, China the main buyer — closely tied to US–China trade and corn.
