# Vegetable Oils: Palm, Rapeseed/Canola, Sunflower

Veg oils are a single substitutable pool (~220+ Mt/yr): palm, soy, rapeseed and sunflower oils compete on price, with biofuel mandates adding energy linkage.

## Palm oil

- **Indonesia (~58%) and Malaysia (~25%)** produce ~85% of world palm oil. Cheapest veg oil by yield per hectare.
- **Seasonality:** output rises Mar–Oct, peaks Sep–Oct, troughs Jan–Feb. A lagged response to rainfall; El Niño droughts cut yields 6–12 months later.
- **Indonesian policy is the dominant driver:** biodiesel mandate (B40 implemented 2025, B50 planned), export levies and DMO rules. Higher mandates remove exportable supply.
- Aging trees and slow replanting limit growth; labour shortages in Malaysia.
- Data: **MPOB** monthly (stocks, production, exports); cargo surveyors (ITS, AmSpec) every 5/10 days.
- Demand: India (largest importer, sensitive to import duty changes), China, EU (EUDR deforestation regulation), Pakistan.

## Rapeseed / canola

- Canada (canola), EU, Australia, Ukraine. EU uses rapeseed oil mainly for biodiesel.
- Canola crush in Canada is expanding for renewable diesel; China–Canada trade tariffs (2025) hit canola meal/oil.

## Sunflower oil

- **Ukraine and Russia ~55–60% of world production and ~75%+ of exports**; war risk premium.

## Key spreads

- **POGO** (palm oil – gasoil): biodiesel blending economics.
- **Soy–palm spread (SBO–CPO)**: when palm discount narrows too much, buyers (India) switch to soy oil.
- Rapeseed–soybean oil spread in the EU.
