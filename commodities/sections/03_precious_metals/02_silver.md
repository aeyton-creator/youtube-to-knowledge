# Silver

Hybrid metal: part monetary (follows gold), part industrial (over half of demand). More volatile than gold — typically 1.5–2× gold's volatility.

## Supply

- Mine supply ~820–830 Moz/yr; ~70% is a **by-product** of lead/zinc, copper and gold mining, so supply does not respond much to silver price.
- Top producers: Mexico, China, Peru, Chile, Bolivia, Poland, Russia.
- Recycling ~180–200 Moz.
- The market has been in **structural deficit** since 2021 (Silver Institute data), drawing down above-ground stocks.

## Demand

- **Industrial (~55–60%)**: electronics, brazing, and especially **photovoltaics** (silver paste in solar cells); thrifting by solar manufacturers is the main counter-risk.
- Jewellery and silverware (India is the largest buyer).
- Investment: coins/bars, ETFs (SLV).

## Price drivers

1. Gold direction and the gold/silver ratio (historically 50–90; extremes above 90 tend to mean-revert).
2. Solar installation growth (China), electronics cycle.
3. Indian import demand and duty changes.
4. Real rates and dollar (via gold).
5. London vault free-float tightness — lease rates spike when available metal is scarce.
