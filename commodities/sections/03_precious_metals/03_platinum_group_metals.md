# Platinum Group Metals (Platinum, Palladium, Rhodium)

Small, concentrated, illiquid markets dominated by autocatalysts and by two producing countries.

## Supply

- **South Africa** (Bushveld Complex): ~70% of platinum, ~80% of rhodium supply. Risks: Eskom power cuts, labour strikes, deep-level mining costs, rand.
- **Russia** (Norilsk Nickel): ~40% of palladium supply (by-product of nickel).
- Zimbabwe, North America are smaller sources.
- Recycling of autocatalysts is significant (~25–30% of supply), and depends on scrap vehicle rates.

## Demand

| Metal | Main uses |
|---|---|
| Platinum | Diesel autocatalysts, heavy-duty vehicles, jewellery, glass/chemical industry, investment, hydrogen fuel cells/electrolysers (PEM) |
| Palladium | Gasoline autocatalysts (~80% of demand) |
| Rhodium | Gasoline autocatalysts (NOx reduction) |

- **Battery EVs carry no autocatalyst** → long-term structural headwind for palladium/rhodium; hybrids carry more PGM loading than ICE.
- **Substitution:** high palladium prices drove platinum-for-palladium substitution in gasoline catalysts.
- Tightening emissions standards (Euro 7, China 6b) raise loadings.

## Trading notes

- Platinum has been in deficit per World Platinum Investment Council data for several years; above-ground stocks cover the shortfall.
- Pt–Pd spread is a classic relative-value trade on substitution and EV penetration.
- Thin liquidity → sharp squeezes; watch NYMEX warehouse stocks and lease rates.
- Sanctions risk on Russian palladium is a perennial tail.
