## Fundamentals

- Short biological cycle: gestation ~114 days, market weight ~6 months after birth → supply responds within ~10 months.
- **Strong seasonality:** prices peak in summer (Jun–Jul) as slaughter is lowest and grilling demand is high, and trough in Nov–Dec when slaughter is highest.
- **Hog crush (margin):** hogs − (corn + soybean meal) feed cost.
- Packer margin: pork cutout vs hog prices; plant capacity limits (Saturday kills).

## Demand

- US domestic, plus exports to Mexico (#1), China, Japan, South Korea, Canada. China's hog herd (largest in the world; African Swine Fever 2018–2020 halved it) swings import needs. Chinese tariffs on US pork are recurring.
- Variety meats (offal) exports to China add value per head.

## Key reports

- Quarterly **Hogs and Pigs** (USDA): inventory, breeding herd, farrowing intentions, pigs per litter.
- Daily slaughter, weights, pork cutout; weekly export sales; cold storage (monthly).
- Disease risk: African Swine Fever (ASF), PRRS, PED.
