## Fundamentals

- **Global Dairy Trade (GDT)** auction every two weeks (Fonterra-led) sets international powder/butter prices.
- Exporters: New Zealand, EU, US. Importers: China, Southeast Asia, Middle East, Mexico.
- Supply drivers: herd size, feed costs (milk–feed ratio), weather/pasture (NZ season Aug–May), disease (H5N1 avian flu in US dairy herds since 2024).
- US Federal Milk Marketing Orders define class prices (Class I fluid, II soft products, III cheese, IV butter/powder); reform in 2025 changed formulas.
