# Carbon & Environmental Markets

## Major compliance markets

| Market | Instrument / venue | Size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| EU ETS | EUA, ICE Endex / EEX | 1,000 t CO2 | December contract is the benchmark; power, industry, aviation, maritime (phased from 2024) |
| UK ETS | UKA, ICE | 1,000 t | Linking with EU ETS under negotiation |
| California / Québec | CCA, ICE | 1,000 t | Quarterly auctions; floor price |
| RGGI (US Northeast) | RGA, ICE | 1,000 t | Power sector |
| China national ETS | Shanghai Environment Exchange | — | Power, expanding to steel, cement, aluminium |

## EU ETS mechanics

- Cap declines annually (linear reduction factor raised under Fit for 55); free allocation phases out as **CBAM** phases in (2026–2034).
- **Market Stability Reserve (MSR)** removes surplus allowances when total number in circulation (TNAC) is high.
- **ETS2** (buildings and road transport) planned from 2027/2028.
- Auction calendar (EEX) and compliance deadline (30 September) shape supply/demand.

## Price drivers

1. Fuel switching: gas vs coal costs — higher gas prices raise coal burn and EUA demand.
2. Industrial output (recession lowers emissions).
3. Renewable output (wind, hydro, solar) displaces fossil power.
4. Policy announcements (MSR changes, REPowerEU sales, cap adjustments).
5. Speculative positioning by investment funds (EU publishes weekly CoT).

## US RINs and LCFS

- **RINs** (Renewable Identification Numbers) under the US RFS: D6 (corn ethanol), D4 (biomass-based diesel), D5 (advanced), D3 (cellulosic). Obligated parties (refiners) must retire RINs. D4 prices link soybean oil to policy.
- **California LCFS** credits: carbon-intensity-based; price sensitive to rulemaking.
