# Freight (Dry Bulk & Tankers)

Shipping costs determine arbitrage between regions; freight can be traded via **Forward Freight Agreements (FFAs)** cleared on SGX and EEX.

## Dry bulk

| Vessel | Size (dwt) | Main cargoes | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capesize | 150,000–200,000+ | Iron ore, coal | Baltic Capesize 5TC; C3 (Tubarão–Qingdao), C5 (W. Australia–Qingdao) |
| Panamax / Kamsarmax | 65,000–85,000 | Coal, grains | Baltic Panamax 5TC/4TC |
| Supramax / Ultramax | 50,000–65,000 | Grains, fertiliser, minor bulks | Baltic Supramax 10TC |
| Handysize | 25,000–40,000 | Minor bulks | Baltic Handysize 7TC |

The **Baltic Dry Index (BDI)** is a weighted composite of these.

Rates quoted in **Worldscale** points (percentage of a notional flat rate per route) or $/day time-charter equivalent (TCE).

## Drivers

1. Tonne-miles: the distance cargoes travel (e.g., Red Sea diversions around the Cape from late 2023 raised tonne-miles sharply).
2. Fleet supply: orderbook, scrapping, IMO environmental rules (CII, EEXI), sanctioned "shadow fleet".
3. Seasonal cargo flows: Brazil soy (Q1–Q2), US Gulf grain (Q4), Australian ore (cyclones Q1).
4. Port congestion, canal restrictions (Panama Canal drought 2023–2024).
5. Bunker fuel prices.
