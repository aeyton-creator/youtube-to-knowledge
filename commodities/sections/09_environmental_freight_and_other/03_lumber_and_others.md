# Lumber, Fertilisers & Other Niche Markets

## Lumber

- **CME Lumber (LBR):** 27,500 board feet of 2×4 softwood, physically delivered (launched 2022 replacing the illiquid random-length contract). Thin liquidity.
- Drivers: US housing starts and repair/remodel demand, Canadian supply (BC mill closures, wildfires, pine beetle), **US duties on Canadian softwood lumber**, interest rates.

## Fertilisers

- **Nitrogen** (urea, ammonia, UAN): made from natural gas → European gas prices set the marginal cost. Chinese export restrictions and Indian import tenders are key.
- **Phosphates** (DAP, MAP): Morocco (OCP), China (export quotas), US (Mosaic), Saudi Arabia (Ma'aden).
- **Potash** (MOP): Canada (Nutrien), Russia, Belarus — highly concentrated; annual China/India contract prices set the tone.
- Venues: CME urea/DAP/UAN swaps (cash-settled vs Argus/CRU/Fertilizer Week). Fertiliser affordability influences corn acreage and yield.

## Other markets to consider adding

- **Rubber** (SGX TSR20, OSE RSS3, SHFE) — Thailand/Indonesia supply; auto tyre demand.
- **Wool, potatoes (EEX), cheese, salmon (Fish Pool / Euronext)**.
- **Rare earths** (NdPr oxide) — Chinese supply and export controls.
- **Molybdenum, vanadium, tungsten, antimony, gallium, germanium** — critical minerals subject to Chinese export controls.
- **Hydrogen, RECs/GOs, weather derivatives (CME HDD/CDD)**.
