## Futures month codes

| Code | Month | Code | Month | Code | Month |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| F | January | K | May | U | September |
| G | February | M | June | V | October |
| H | March | N | July | X | November |
| J | April | Q | August | Z | December |

Example: **CLZ6** = WTI crude December 2026; **ZSX6** = soybeans November 2026.

## Seasonal calendar overview

| Period | Typical focus |
|---|---|
| Jan–Feb | South American soy harvest begins; US winter gas draws peak; Chinese New Year metals destock |
| Mar–Apr | USDA Prospective Plantings; Brazil safrinha corn planting; refinery maintenance ends; LME Week Asia |
| May–Jun | US planting/emergence; Atlantic hurricane season opens (1 Jun); driving season begins |
| Jul–Aug | Corn pollination (July) and soybean pod-set (August) — peak weather premium; peak US power burn |
| Sep–Oct | US harvest; Brazil soy planting; West African cocoa main crop starts (Oct); LME Week London |
| Nov–Dec | Gas withdrawal season starts; Southern Hemisphere wheat harvest; index rebalancing in January |
