## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Backwardation | Prompt price above deferred price; signals scarcity |
| Basis | Cash price minus futures price at a location |
| Contango | Deferred price above prompt; signals surplus/storage economics |
| Convenience yield | Implicit benefit of holding physical inventory |
| Crack spread | Refining margin (products minus crude) |
| Crush spread | Processing margin (soy products minus beans; hogs/cattle minus feed) |
| Differential | Premium/discount of a physical grade/origin vs benchmark |
| EFP | Exchange for physical — swaps a futures position for a physical one |
| FND | First notice day: earliest date delivery notices may be issued |
| LTD | Last trading day |
| Open interest | Number of open futures contracts |
| OSP | Official selling price set by national oil companies |
| Spark/dark spread | Power margin over gas/coal generation |
| Stocks-to-use | Ending stocks ÷ annual use; key ag tightness metric |
| TC/RC | Treatment/refining charges paid by miners to smelters |
| Warrant | Title document for metal in an exchange warehouse |
| WASDE | USDA World Agricultural Supply and Demand Estimates |
