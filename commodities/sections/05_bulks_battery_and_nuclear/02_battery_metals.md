# Battery Metals: Lithium, Cobalt, Graphite, Manganese

Young, opaque markets with limited exchange liquidity. Prices are set by price-reporting agencies (Fastmarkets, Benchmark, SMM) and Chinese futures.

## Lithium

- **Supply:** hard rock (spodumene) — Australia (Greenbushes, Pilgangoora); brines — Chile (SQM, Albemarle), Argentina (rapid growth); **lepidolite** in Jiangxi, China (high-cost marginal supply); Zimbabwe; Africa growing. China dominates chemical conversion (~65%+).
- **Demand:** EV batteries (~75%+), energy storage (ESS — fastest-growing), consumer electronics, ceramics/glass.
- **Price cycle:** extreme boom in 2021–2022 followed by a collapse of more than 80% by 2024–2025 on supply overbuild. Mine closures and Chinese lepidolite permit issues (e.g., CATL Jianxiawo 2025) drive rallies.
- Units: LCE (lithium carbonate equivalent); spodumene concentrate ~6% Li2O (SC6). Rule of thumb: ~7.5–8 t SC6 per t LCE.

## Cobalt

- **DRC supplies ~75%** of mined cobalt, mostly as a copper by-product (CMOC, Glencore). China refines ~75%+.
- Demand: batteries (NMC, NCA), superalloys (aerospace), hard metals.
- **DRC export ban (Feb 2025)** then quota system — a supply-policy-driven market. LFP batteries (cobalt-free) cap long-term demand growth.
- Artisanal mining and ESG/child-labour concerns affect Western buyer sourcing.

## Graphite and manganese

- Graphite (anode material): China dominates natural and synthetic graphite processing; Chinese export controls (since Dec 2023) are a key risk.
- Manganese: steel (~90%) and LMFP/NMC battery cathodes; South Africa, Gabon, Australia.

## Trading notes

- Watch Chinese EV sales (CPCA weekly/monthly), battery production (CABIA), ESS tenders, GFEX open interest, Australian miner quarterlies, Chilean/Argentine export data.
- Policy (China export controls, US IRA/FEOC rules, DRC quotas) can override fundamentals.
