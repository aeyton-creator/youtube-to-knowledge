# Tin

The smallest and most volatile LME base metal (~380 kt/yr), with highly concentrated and fragile supply.

## Contract specifications

| Contract | Venue | Size |
|---|---|---|
| LME Tin | LME SN | 5 t |
| SHFE Tin | SHFE SN | 1 t |

## Fundamentals

- **Demand:** solder (~50%) — electronics and semiconductors; tinplate (packaging); chemicals; lead-acid battery additives. Semiconductor/AI cycle is the key demand driver.
- **Supply:** China, Indonesia (export licence/policy changes, crackdown on illegal mining), Myanmar (Wa State — mining suspended from 2023, slow restart), Peru, DRC (Bisie mine — conflict risk), Bolivia, Brazil.
- Very low LME stocks relative to consumption → violent squeezes and backwardations.

## Trading notes

- Watch Indonesian export data (monthly), Myanmar Wa State news, Chinese smelter feedstock (concentrate imports), Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX).
- Liquidity is thin; size positions conservatively.
