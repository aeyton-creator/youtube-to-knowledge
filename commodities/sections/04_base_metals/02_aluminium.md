# Aluminium

The most consumed non-ferrous metal (~70 Mt/yr primary). Production is an energy-intensive process — "congealed electricity" — so power costs drive the cost curve.

## Value chain

- **Bauxite → alumina → aluminium**: ~4–5 t bauxite → ~2 t alumina → 1 t aluminium.
- Smelting uses ~13–15 MWh per tonne; electricity is ~35–40% of cost.
- Bauxite: Guinea, Australia, China; alumina: China, Australia; aluminium: **China ~60% of world output**.

## Supply dynamics

- China imposed a **45 Mt capacity cap** — growth is shifting to Indonesia, India, the Middle East.
- Yunnan hydro shortages periodically curtail Chinese output in the dry season.
- European smelters curtailed in 2022 on power costs; restarts are slow.
- Russian supply (Rusal) faces sanctions; can't be delivered on LME (post-April 2024 production).

## Demand

- Transport (lightweighting, EVs), construction, packaging (cans), electrical (grid cables), consumer durables.
- Recycling (secondary aluminium) uses ~5% of the energy of primary.

## Price drivers

1. Energy costs (Chinese coal, European power).
2. Chinese production vs capacity cap; exports of semi-finished products.
3. Trade policy: US Section 232 tariffs, EU CBAM (carbon border adjustment, definitive phase from 2026), anti-dumping duties.
4. Alumina price spikes (2024 spike on bauxite and refinery outages).
5. Regional premiums — the **US Midwest premium** rises with tariffs.
