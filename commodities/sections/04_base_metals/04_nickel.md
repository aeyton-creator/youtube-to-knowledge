# Nickel

A market transformed by Indonesia, which grew from a small producer to more than half of global supply in under a decade.

## Product types

- **Class 1** (≥99.8%): cathodes, briquettes, powder — LME deliverable; used in batteries (via sulphate) and alloys.
- **Class 2** (<99.8%): **nickel pig iron (NPI)** and ferronickel — used in stainless steel.
- **Intermediates:** mixed hydroxide precipitate (MHP), nickel matte — feed for battery sulphate.

## Supply

- **Indonesia (>50%)**: built on Chinese-financed NPI and HPAL (high-pressure acid leach) plants after the 2020 nickel ore export ban. Government RKAB mining quotas are now a key policy lever.
- Philippines (ore exports), Russia (Norilsk — Class 1), Canada, Australia (many mines closed in 2024 due to low prices), New Caledonia (crisis, unrest).

## Demand

- **Stainless steel ~65–70%** (China dominant).
- **Batteries ~15%** and growing (NMC chemistries) — but LFP batteries (no nickel) have taken share.
- Alloys, plating.

## Price drivers

1. Indonesian supply policy (quotas, royalties, export restrictions).
2. Chinese stainless output and margins.
3. EV battery chemistry mix (NMC vs LFP).
4. LME stocks: Class 1 from Indonesia/China now registered as LME brands, raising deliverable stocks.

## Trading notes

- The LME price reflects only Class 1, yet most production is Class 2 → disconnect between LME and the bulk of the physical market; watch NPI prices and premiums.
- The 2022 short squeeze reduced LME liquidity for years; daily limits apply.
