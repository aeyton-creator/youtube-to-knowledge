## Zinc

- ~13–14 Mt/yr. Main use: **galvanising steel (~50–60%)**, die-casting alloys, brass, chemicals.
- Demand tied to construction, infrastructure, autos — very China-centric.
- Mine supply: China, Peru, Australia, India, US, Mexico. Smelting dominated by China.
- **Zinc TC/RCs** are a key signal (annual benchmark negotiated between miners and smelters; spot TCs move monthly).
- European smelters are sensitive to energy prices (Nyrstar, Glencore curtailments 2022).

## Lead

- ~12–13 Mt/yr. **Lead-acid batteries ~80%** of demand (automotive starter batteries, backup power, e-bikes in Asia).
- Over 60% of supply is **secondary** (recycled batteries) — making lead the most recycled metal. Supply is therefore stable and price-insensitive.
- Replacement-battery demand is weather-sensitive (cold winters, hot summers kill batteries).
- Long-term headwind from lithium-ion substitution, but ICE and EV 12V systems still need lead-acid.
